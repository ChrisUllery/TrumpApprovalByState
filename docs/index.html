About this project

Source & methodology

National overview: On first load, the sidebar displays Civiqs's separately reported nationwide estimates, not an average of state estimates. The national and state reporting dates appear in their respective views and may differ when updated at different times. National and state approval estimates describe registered voters; 2024 popular-vote shares describe actual presidential votes cast.

Polling source: Civiqs — Donald Trump: Job Approval, Second Term. Values shown are rounded modeled estimates as displayed on the Civiqs results page, not results of 50 independent state polls. See Civiqs's methodology. Civiqs estimates are based on sampled survey responses and statistical modeling; they do not represent interviews with every registered voter in a state.

Map views: All three views show net approval, calculated as approval minus disapproval, in percentage points (pp). A % sign is used beside net values as a familiar display convention, but the calculation is a difference in percentage points. Overall uses all registered voters; Republican and Democratic views measure net approval within each party for the selected state. The party maps use their own labeled, data-driven color ranges to make small differences visible; colors should not be compared across different map views.

Rounding: All net values are derived from the displayed approval and disapproval estimates, not Civiqs's unrounded model, and may differ slightly from Civiqs's published net values. When Civiqs shows <1%, its source label is preserved and 0.5% is used as a computational placeholder. All derived net figures are rounded to the nearest whole number for display, with halves rounded away from zero. These values remain approximate. Percentages may not total 100 because of rounding.

2024 election results: Trump's 2024 popular-vote share is calculated as his votes divided by all presidential votes reported in that state, including third-party and reported write-in votes, times 100. Vote totals come from the Federal Election Commission's official 2024 presidential results. The FEC denominator is ballots cast for president; Civiqs estimates opinions among registered voters at a later date. The two measures are not directly interchangeable. The won/lost wording is based on statewide popular-vote results, not district-level electoral vote allocations.

Geography: Boundaries from U.S. Atlas, derived from U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary data. The dataset covers 50 states, not Washington, D.C. or territories. This dashboard is an independently designed visualization, not an official Civiqs product.
